Privacy Policy - Quest Academy Factory

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Quest Academy Factory is an internal tool operated by MR Quest Technologies (Bengaluru, India) to upload Quest Academy's own educational videos to its own YouTube channel.

What data the app accesses

The app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, thumbnails and captions, and to create and manage playlists on the Quest Academy channel only. It is authorized solely by the channel owner.

What we store

We store only an OAuth refresh token, kept as an encrypted secret in our build system, and the IDs of the videos and playlists we create. We do not collect, store, sell or share data about any other users.

Third parties

Data is sent only to Google/YouTube through the YouTube API Services. Use of this app is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

The channel owner can revoke access at any time from Google account permissions.
