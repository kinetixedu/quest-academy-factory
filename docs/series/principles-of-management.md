## The running story (canon - keep it consistent across every episode)
Every episode is told through **Chai Corner**, a chai stall run by **Ravi** that slowly grows into a small chain. Viewers follow the same people episode after episode, so they come back for the next one.
- **Ravi** - owner and manager. Navy bust with a gold collar, gold "R" avatar.
- **Asha** - makes the tea (cup icon). Later becomes head chef / kitchen lead.
- **Kabir** - handles money and the cash box (coin icon). Later runs accounts.
- **Meena** - washes cups, keeps the stall clean (droplet icon). Later runs operations at the second outlet.
- Growth arc you may use, in order: one stall (Ep 1-6) -> a busier stall with 6 staff (Ep 7-13, the history episodes compare how Taylor, Fayol, Weber, Mayo would run Chai Corner) -> a second outlet (Ep 14-20) -> Chai Corner with 5 outlets and a small head office (Ep 21-28: decisions, delegation, centralization, span of control, effectiveness).
- Settled canon so far: the second outlet is at the BUS STAND (chosen in Ep 15) and opened in Ep 14; Meena runs it with two new helpers; Asha trains new staff; Ravi stays the owner. Two unnamed helpers joined the first stall around Ep 7 (show as plain user icons, no names). From Ep 21 there are 5 outlets and a small head office.
- Never introduce real brands into the story. Real thinkers (Taylor, Fayol, Weber, Mayo, Drucker, Simon, Follett) are fine, with correct facts and dates.
- Illustrative numbers are labelled with the ghost chip "EXAMPLE FIGURES" in the header row.

## Every episode must have
1. Eyebrow chip: `EP NN · PRINCIPLES OF MANAGEMENT` (two digits). Title card -> header travel exactly as Ep 01.
2. Hook in the first 5 s built on a Chai Corner problem the topic solves.
3. One clear definition card (quote + attribution if the definition is a known thinker's; otherwise a plain sentence).
4. The core concept built step by step with the MBA components (cards, ring/cycle, rows, before/after bars, navy statement sweep for one punch line).
5. Quick check (4 options, 3-2-1 ring, answer reveal, takeaway strip), then recap ("Remember these three things", 3 rows).
6. Final strip: `NEXT · EP NN+1` + next topic title (from the queue's next_topic). Last episode (Ep 28) instead says "Series complete" and points to the playlist.
7. 2-3 minutes. 12-year-old language. No em/en dashes anywhere.
8. meta.json includes `"series": {"name": "Principles of Management", "ep": N}`; title format: `<Hook-style title> Explained Simply | Principles of Management Ep N` (<= 70 chars, shorten the hook part if needed). Description names the episode number and the next episode.
