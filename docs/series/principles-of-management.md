## The running story (canon - keep it consistent across every episode)
Every episode is told through **Chai Corner**, a chai stall run by **Ravi** that slowly grows into a small chain. Viewers follow the same people episode after episode, so they come back for the next one.
- **Ravi** - owner and manager. Navy bust with a gold collar, gold "R" avatar.
- **Asha** - makes the tea (cup icon). Later becomes head chef / kitchen lead.
- **Kabir** - handles money and the cash box (coin icon). Later runs accounts.
- **Meena** - washes cups, keeps the stall clean (droplet icon). Later runs operations at the second outlet.
- Growth arc you may use, in order: one stall (Ep 1-6) -> a busier stall with 6 staff (Ep 7-13, the history episodes compare how Taylor, Fayol, Weber, Mayo would run Chai Corner) -> a second outlet (Ep 14-20) -> Chai Corner with 5 outlets and a small head office (Ep 21-28: decisions, delegation, centralization, span of control, effectiveness).
- Settled canon so far: the second outlet is at the BUS STAND (chosen in Ep 15) and opened in Ep 14; Meena runs it with two new helpers; Asha trains new staff; Ravi stays the owner. Two unnamed helpers joined the first stall around Ep 7 (show as plain user icons, no names). From Ep 21 there are 5 outlets and a small head office.
- Never introduce real brands into the story. Real thinkers (Taylor, Fayol, Weber, Mayo, Drucker, Simon, Follett) are fine, with correct facts and dates.
- Illustrative numbers are labelled with the ghost chip "EXAMPLE FIGURES" in the header row.

## Every episode must have (v2 standard, approved on Ep 08)
1. Eyebrow chip: `EP NN · PRINCIPLES OF MANAGEMENT` (two digits). Title card -> header travel exactly as Ep 01.
2. Hook in the first 5 s built on a Chai Corner problem the topic solves.
3. **Illustrated introduction / storytelling**: the thinker's portrait card if the topic has one (Fayol, Weber, Mayo, Follett, Drucker, Simon), otherwise a Chai Corner scene from the library. Historical scenes where the topic has a real origin story. Names and dates as Poppins text, never in the image.
4. One clear definition card (quote + attribution if it is a known thinker's definition; otherwise a plain sentence).
5. **Animate the actual process at least once**: show the concept happening step by step with motion, timers, flows or a before/after run (Ep 08: Meena's way vs Asha's way on a floor plan with a stopwatch). Not cards alone.
6. The core concept with precise diagrams (cards, ring/cycle, rows, bars, timelines, org charts) and one navy statement sweep.
7. **At work today**: for each key element/principle, one Chai Corner example + one real workplace example (generic, no brand names). Ep 08 list + pane layout.
8. **The human side / limits**: a short balanced section - what it gains, what it costs or where it fails, with a fact if one exists. End on a one-line lesson.
9. Quick check (4 options, 3-2-1 ring, answer reveal, takeaway strip), then recap ("Remember these three things", 3 rows).
10. Final strip: `NEXT · EP NN+1` + next topic title. Ep 28 instead says "Series complete" and points to the playlist.
11. **3:00 - 4:00** of voice. 12-year-old language, MBA-level substance. No em/en dashes anywhere.
12. meta.json includes `"series": {"name": "Principles of Management", "ep": N}`; title format: `<Hook-style title> Explained Simply | Principles of Management Ep N` (<= 70 chars). Description names the episode number, has chapter timestamps and the next episode.
