# Overrides for PUC Biology episodes (apply on top of docs/series/agent-brief.md)

- Standard: docs/series/PUC.md ("Every PUC episode must have", "Ending order", shared editing style, puc-biology "Living diagram") and the frames docs/series/puc-samples/4-biology-lesson.png and 4-biology-exam.png. Where agent-brief.md (MBA) and PUC.md differ, PUC.md wins: no running story, no "At work today", no human-side section, no 3-2-1 quick check; instead the PUC exam segment (trap, where asked, MCQ with 5-second ring, animated solution, fact card).
- Canon, chapter numbers and episode list: docs/series/puc-biology.md. Never change a chapter number or name from that file.
- Approved reference for THIS series: the PUC Biology Ep 1 folder named in docs/series/puc-biology.md (after Amjad approves it). Copy its head/CSS block, helpers and components. Engine, captions, title travel, pacing, outro call and quality bar come from videos/mba-introduction-to-marketing/ and videos/mba-scientific-management-f-w-taylor/.
- World: worlds/puc/ (shared, built by the PUC Physics session). Reuse it, never edit it. Only the accent changes: leaf green #3E9B5F, subject pill LIVING DIAGRAM. Cast images: assets/illustrations/puc/ (shared, read only). Own images only as assets/illustrations/puc/puc-biology-*, never overwriting shared files.
- Look: graph paper #F7F5EF with 40 px grid #E9E5DA, ink #16213E, white cards with a 6 px green top edge, Poppins 700 headings / 500 body, IBM Plex Mono for every number, size, count and scale chip, KaTeX (bundled locally) for every equation (photosynthesis, respiration, ratios), chemical formulas upright (\mathrm{}).
- Living diagram vocabulary: textbook-quality labelled diagrams (SVG or label-free cutouts) with labels that pop in on leader lines drawn from the part to the label; zoom ladder chip row (organ -> tissue -> cell -> organelle -> molecule) with one smooth push-in per level and a scale chip ("x400"); processes as stage chains with the current stage lit and a looping animation inside it. NCERT diagram names exactly. Signature: zoom ladder + the labelled diagrams NEET asks about.
- Colour-coding: the fixed colours in the bible ("Living diagram canon") are the same in the diagram, the label dot, the equation and the readout (chlorophyll green, mitochondrion/ATP orange, DNA violet, water sky, CO2 grey, glucose gold).
- Motion: one thing moves at a time, labels and lines draw on, 0.6-0.8 s script pause after each mechanism step, camera push-in max 1.15x per scene except the zoom ladder (each rung is its own scene step, scale chip shows the real magnification idea, never claim a real microscope value unless verified), no particles, glows or 3D.
- Transitions: notebook page turn with a green strip on the page edge only for the statement, the exam segment (quiz) and the fact card; elsewhere vis/rise.
- Sounds: only M.sfx(t,'whoosh',0.22) on page turns, 3 ticks (0.28) in the MCQ countdown, 'correct' (0.32) on the answer. Nothing else.
- Cast: hook only (Diya for most topics, Aarav where listed per episode in the bible), then small face avatars with one-line reactions. The concept carries the video.
- Eyebrow / NCERT chip: `CLASS 11 · BIOLOGY · CH N CHAPTER NAME` exactly as in the bible. Title: `<Hook-style title> Explained Simply | PUC Biology Class 11` (Class 12 for second-year topics), <= 70 chars. meta.json "series": {"name": "PUC Biology", "ep": N}.
- Exam segment: "Where it is asked" chips light only exams verified from a cited past-paper analysis; if not verifiable, light none and state only the question type. MCQ is ORIGINAL (never copied), real NEET level (statement-based, match-the-following, assertion-reason or diagram-label patterns are fine), 4 options, answer and every solution step checked against NCERT.
- Facts: NCERT first for every name, number, value and date. Never invent exam weightage, counts or statistics. Illustrative numbers carry the ghost chip "EXAMPLE FIGURES".
- Length 4:00 to 6:00 of voice. 12-year-old language, NEET substance underneath. Plain hyphens only. No real brands.
- If you introduce a recurring fact (a colour for a structure, a notation choice), state it in your report so the lead adds it to the bible.
