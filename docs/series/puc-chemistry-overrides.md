# Overrides for PUC Chemistry episodes (apply on top of docs/series/agent-brief.md)

- Standard: docs/series/PUC.md ("Every PUC episode must have", "Ending order", shared editing style, puc-chemistry "Molecule bench") and the frames docs/series/puc-samples/2-chemistry-lesson.png and 2-chemistry-exam.png. Where agent-brief.md (MBA) and PUC.md differ, PUC.md wins: no running story, no "At work today", no human-side section, no 3-2-1 quick check; instead the PUC exam segment (trap, where asked, MCQ with 5-second ring, formula card).
- Canon, chapter numbers and episode list: docs/series/puc-chemistry.md. Never change a chapter number or name from that file.
- Approved reference for THIS series: the PUC Chemistry Ep 1 folder named in docs/series/puc-chemistry.md (after Amjad approves it). Copy its head/CSS block, helpers and components. Engine, captions, title travel, pacing, outro call and quality bar come from videos/mba-introduction-to-marketing/ and videos/mba-scientific-management-f-w-taylor/.
- World: worlds/puc/ (shared, built by the PUC Physics session). Reuse it, never edit it. Only the accent changes: teal #0FA3A3. Cast images: assets/illustrations/puc/ (shared, read only). Own images only as assets/illustrations/puc/puc-chemistry-* if ever needed, never overwriting shared files.
- Look: graph paper #F7F5EF with 40 px grid #E9E5DA, ink #16213E, white cards with a 6 px teal top edge, Poppins 700 headings / 500 body, IBM Plex Mono for every number and readout, KaTeX (bundled locally) for every formula, variables italic, units upright, chemical symbols upright (\mathrm{}).
- Colour-coding: every symbol in a formula takes the colour of the thing it stands for in the diagram (the moving electron e- is the same orange in the bench, the equation and the readout; the element in play is teal on the periodic strip and in the formula).
- Molecule bench vocabulary: ball-and-stick molecules that assemble bond by bond; electron dots that move between atoms on thin orbit rings; reaction arrows that draw on; live balance counter (atoms and charge, left | right, ticks when equal); energy-profile curves that draw as the reaction runs; flat beakers; periodic table strip lighting the element in play. Signature: electrons visibly move to explain every bond and reaction.
- Motion: one thing moves at a time, formulas and lines draw on stroke by stroke, 0.6-0.8 s script pause after each derivation step, camera push-in max 1.15x once per scene, no particles, glows or 3D.
- Transitions: notebook page turn with a teal strip on the page edge only for the statement, the exam segment (quiz) and the formula card; elsewhere vis/rise.
- Sounds: only M.sfx(t,'whoosh',0.22) on page turns, 3 ticks (0.28) in the MCQ countdown, 'correct' (0.32) on the answer. Nothing else.
- Cast: hook only (Diya for most topics, Aarav for industrial and electrochemistry topics, as listed per episode in the bible), then small face avatars with one-line reactions. The concept carries the video.
- Eyebrow / NCERT chip: `CLASS 11 · CHEMISTRY · CH N CHAPTER NAME` exactly as in the bible. Title: `<Hook-style title> Explained Simply | PUC Chemistry Class 11` (Class 12 for second-year topics), <= 70 chars. meta.json "series": {"name": "PUC Chemistry", "ep": N}.
- Exam segment: "Where it is asked" chips light only exams verified from a cited past-paper analysis; if not verifiable, light none and state only the question type. MCQ is ORIGINAL (never copied), real JEE/NEET level, 4 options, answer and every solution step checked by hand and by running the arithmetic.
- Facts: NCERT first for every constant, value, name and date. Never invent exam weightage, counts or statistics. Illustrative numbers carry the ghost chip "EXAMPLE FIGURES".
- Length 4:00 to 6:00 of voice. 12-year-old language, JEE/NEET substance underneath. Plain hyphens only. No real brands.
- If you introduce a recurring fact (a constant value, a notation choice, a colour for a symbol), state it in your report so the lead adds it to the bible.
