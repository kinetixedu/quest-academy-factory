# Overrides for PUC Maths episodes (apply on top of docs/series/agent-brief.md)

- Standard: docs/series/PUC.md ("Every PUC episode must have", "Ending order", shared editing style, puc-maths "Proof board") and the frames docs/series/puc-samples/3-maths-lesson.png and 3-maths-exam.png. Where agent-brief.md (MBA) and PUC.md differ, PUC.md wins: no running story, no "At work today", no human-side section, no 3-2-1 quick check; instead the PUC exam segment (trap, where asked, MCQ with 5-second ring, formula card).
- Canon, chapter numbers, hooks, colour-coding and episode list: docs/series/puc-maths.md. Never change a chapter number or name from that file. Ch 1 note: Power sets and the n(A ∪ B) applications are not in the current NCERT text (see the bible).
- Approved reference for THIS series: videos/puc-maths-definition-and-representation-of-sets/ (Ep 1, after Amjad approves it). Copy its index.html head and stage skeleton, scene.js structure, helpers and components. Engine, captions, title travel, pacing, outro call and quality bar come from videos/mba-introduction-to-marketing/ and videos/mba-scientific-management-f-w-taylor/.
- World: worlds/puc/ (puc.css, puc.js, KaTeX; read worlds/puc/README.md for the PUC.* API). Never edit worlds/, engine/ or tools/. Cast and scenes: assets/illustrations/puc/ (read only). New images only as assets/illustrations/puc/puc-maths-* with a line in the README's PUC Maths section.
- Look: graph paper #F7F5EF with 40 px grid, ink #16213E, white cards with a 6 px coral top edge, Poppins 700 / 500, IBM Plex Mono for every number, count and readout, KaTeX for every formula and set (variables italic).
- Proof board: equations write on (PUC.write); each new step slides in under the previous one and the changed term is highlighted then settles; every algebra step paired with its picture (number line, Venn region, grid, triangle, parabola) whose parts take the colour of their terms; a live parameter that counts up while the picture morphs; worked solutions in a numbered column, never more than 5 lines on screen.
- Motion: one thing moves at a time, 0.6-0.8 s script pause after each derivation step, camera push-in max 1.15x once per scene, no particles, glows or 3D.
- Transitions: notebook page turn (PUC.sweepRun) only for the statement, the exam segment (MCQ) and the formula card; elsewhere M.vis / M.rise.
- Sounds: only M.sfx(t,'whoosh',0.22) on page turns, PUC.mcqSfx (3 ticks + correct). Nothing else. Brand outro via M.outro({}).
- Cast: hook only (lead per the bible: scene card + cutout or face + a one-line puzzle), then small face avatars with one-line reactions. The concept carries the video.
- NCERT chip `CLASS 11 · MATHEMATICS · CH N CHAPTER NAME` exactly as in the bible; subject pill PROOF BOARD. Title `<Hook-style title> Explained Simply | PUC Maths Class 11`, <= 70 chars. meta.json "series": {"name": "PUC Maths", "ep": N}.
- Exam segment: "Where it is asked" chips light only exams verified from a cited past-paper source (cite it in your report); if not verifiable, light none and state only the type of question. NEET has no maths paper: never light NEET. MCQ is ORIGINAL (never copied), real JEE Main level, 4 options; check the answer and every step by hand AND by brute force in python (enumerate the set).
- Facts: NCERT first for every definition and symbol. Never invent exam weightage, counts or statistics. Illustrative numbers carry the ghost chip "EXAMPLE FIGURES".
- Episode shape (Amjad's feedback, see the bible): curiosity opening in the first 10-15 s, reasoning shown step by step, 1-2 PUC.think pauses after side-by-side comparisons, ending = recap card -> three self-check questions -> NEXT teaser. No left border.
- Length 4:00 to 6:00 of voice. 12-year-old language, JEE substance underneath. Plain hyphens only. No real brands.
- Report any recurring fact you introduce (a colour for a symbol, a notation, a named classmate) so the lead adds it to the bible.
