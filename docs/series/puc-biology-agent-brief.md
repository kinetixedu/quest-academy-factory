## Make, per episode: videos/<slug>/ script.txt, index.html, scene.js, meta.json, review/stills.png
Slug = `puc-biology-` + the topic as in queue/topics.csv (grep it there; use that exact slug).
1. **Facts first.** Read the NCERT section for the topic (ncert.nic.in PDFs may be blocked: use sathee.iitk.ac.in NCERT text pages, ciet.nic.in e-texts, NCERT exemplar, or reputable reproductions of the NCERT text) and check every name, number, example organism and date against two sources. Use NCERT names and spellings exactly (e.g. "Theodore Schwann", "Mangifera indica"). For topics marked * in the bible (not in the current NCERT text): keep the chapter chip, add a ghost chip "NOT IN THE CURRENT NCERT TEXT" on the title card and the first lesson card, follow the older NCERT edition / standard texts, never say "NCERT says", and light NO exam chips. If a fact cannot be confirmed, leave it out.
2. **script.txt** (`ID|pause_after_seconds|text`), **4:00 to 6:00** of voice (aim 5:00-5:45; trim if over 6:00). Order (same as the reference): curiosity opening in the first 10-15 s linking 2-3 everyday cases (title-card tiles) -> cast hook (lead per bible; one hook scene card + face, or a hand-built SVG scene) -> definition / core idea built visually -> the Living diagram: textbook-quality labelled SVG diagrams in the Ep 1 vintage palette (labels pop on leader lines drawn from the part, NCERT names), a zoom ladder (organ -> tissue -> cell -> organelle -> molecule, or organism -> part -> cell for animals/microbes) where the topic allows, and at least one REAL animated process (a stage chain with the current stage lit and a looping animation inside it, a life cycle running, a cell dividing, a classification key sorting organisms, a virus infecting a cell...) -> mechanism / reasoning in small steps (each its own line, pause 0.75, every term explained) -> a side-by-side comparison with one simple example each, then one PUC.think (pause 3.2; optionally a second) -> a page-turn statement line "Remember this. ..." (pause 0.6) -> one fully worked example (a labelled diagram or a sort/flow worked through) -> exam trap X1-X3 (wrong struck, right ticks) -> E1 where it is asked -> ORIGINAL NEET-pattern MCQ `Q1` (pause 5.4; statement-based, match-the-following, assertion-reason or diagram-label patterns are good) then `A1..A3` solution lines -> `F1` "Here is your recap. Screenshot this card." (pause 3.2) -> `Y1..Y4` "Can you answer these three questions without help?" -> `N1` "Next time: <curiosity teaser>? We find out in <next topic>." 12-year-old words, NEET substance. Explain every new word the moment it appears. Never open with a definition.
   Then voice it and read cues.json for line times.
3. **index.html + scene.js**: start from copies of the reference files and replace content scenes. NCERT chip exactly as the bible (e.g. `CLASS 11 · BIOLOGY · CH 2 BIOLOGICAL CLASSIFICATION`); subject pill LIVING DIAGRAM; title = short topic (accent word green; the header travel scales it); `#sub` a short variant like "Explained with one living diagram". Rules:
   - Every beat timed from M.t0/M.t1/M.wt (never hardcoded seconds). Check that M.wt word matches are the word you mean (startsWith matching: 'a' matches 'answer'; use an index like wt('A1', 3) when needed). Something meaningful changes every 2-4 s; no dead frames; no card sitting empty for more than ~1.5 s (bring a card in when its content arrives).
   - Layout: safe x 160-1760, content y 260-860, header chip top 96, title top ~156, captions at top 944 never cover content, 32 px gutters (two cards = 784 px), >= 32 px padding, nothing overlaps or clips (diagram cards use overflow hidden: keep labels inside), top-right 140x110 clear. Single-line text nowrap and must fit. Avoid ids that clash with world ids (#st1, #st2, #stF, #stmt, #sweep, #cap, #prog, #title, #sub, #rule).
   - Colour-coding: one colour per structure, same in diagram, label dot, KaTeX and recap (canon in the bible: chlorophyll/chloroplast green, mitochondrion orange, nucleus/DNA violet, water sky, RBC red, sugar gold). Keep a new colour consistent within your batch.
   - Camera push-in at most 1.08-1.1x, once per scene, and never so far that a card or the header is cut badly (prefer pushing into a single card's diagram inside the card, like the zoom ladder).
   - Sounds: only the reference's (whoosh on the 3 page turns, PUC.mcqSfx). M.outro({}) at the end.
   - NEXT strip label `NEXT · EP NN` (two digits) with the next topic from the bible and a curiosity teaser. Ep 39 is the last episode in the current list: its strip label is `PART 1 COMPLETE` with the text "Diversity in the Living World: all four chapters done", and N1 says the unit is complete and points to the playlist (no invented next topic).
4. **Exam segment honesty**: "Where it is asked" lights NO chips unless you verified that exam asked it from a cited past-paper analysis (cite it in your report); otherwise `on: []` and give only the type of question. Never invent weightage or counts. MCQ: original, real NEET level, 4 options, distractors from real mistakes, answer checked against NCERT; the solution lines must match the script.
5. **Check with stills until clean** (most important): sheets of 9 times covering every scene, mid-transitions, the process at several moments, think, statement, worked example, trap, MCQ (before, ring, answer), recap, questions, NEXT. LOOK at every sheet. Fix overlaps, clipping, misalignment, sparse or dead frames, captions over content, unreadable small text, console errors and "word not found" warnings. Expect 3-5 rounds. Final: an 18-still sheet saved to `videos/<slug>/review/stills.png`.
6. **meta.json** like the reference: world "puc-biology", topic, series {"name": "PUC Biology", "ep": N}, title `<Hook-style title> Explained Simply | PUC Biology Class 11` (<= 70 chars, check with python), description (2 short paragraphs on the hook and what the viewer sees, "This is Episode N of our PUC Biology series.", "Made for PUC (Karnataka), CBSE Class 11 and NEET students.", chapter timestamps m:ss from cues.json, "Next episode: ...", "Video by Quest Academy."), 12-14 specific tags, thumb {title 2-4 words, accent_word, frame_t where the labelled diagram is fully built}. Run thumb.js (cpu.sh), LOOK at thumbnail.jpg, fix if weak, then delete videos/<slug>/thumb_frame.jpg and thumbnail.jpg.
