# Overrides for PUC Electronics episodes (apply on top of docs/series/agent-brief.md)

- Standard: docs/series/PUC.md ("Every PUC episode must have", "Ending order", shared editing style, puc-electronics "Breadboard") and the frames docs/series/puc-samples/5-electronics-lesson.png and 5-electronics-exam.png. Where agent-brief.md (MBA) and PUC.md differ, PUC.md wins: no running story, no "At work today", no human-side section, no 3-2-1 quick check; instead the PUC exam segment (trap, where asked, MCQ with 5-second ring, formula card).
- Canon, chapter numbers, colours and the episode list: docs/series/puc-electronics.md. Never change a chapter number or name from that file. Chip: `CLASS 11 · ELECTRONICS · CH N CHAPTER NAME`.
- Approved reference for THIS series: videos/puc-electronics-meaning-scope-and-development-of-electronics/ (Ep 1, after Amjad approves). Copy its index.html head/CSS block, scene.js helpers (schematic builder, electron-dot flow, oscilloscope panel, readout, timeline, steps) and components. Engine, captions, title travel, pacing, outro call and quality bar come from videos/mba-introduction-to-marketing/ and videos/mba-scientific-management-f-w-taylor/.
- World: worlds/puc/ (shared, built by the PUC Physics session). Reuse it, never edit it. Accent amber #E8A317. Cast images: assets/illustrations/puc/ (shared, read only). Own images only as assets/illustrations/puc/puc-electronics-* if ever needed.
- Look: graph paper #F7F5EF with 40 px grid #E9E5DA, ink #16213E, white cards with a 6 px amber top edge, Poppins 700 headings / 500 body, IBM Plex Mono for every number and readout, KaTeX (bundled locally) for every formula, variables italic, units upright (\text{A}, \text{C}).
- Breadboard vocabulary: standard-symbol schematics that draw on stroke by stroke; electrons as amber dots moving along wires, speed proportional to current, - to + outside the source; conventional current as a violet arrow I; live readout (Plex Mono) beside the circuit; dark oscilloscope panel drawing the waveform live (CH1 input, CH2 output); truth tables that fill row by row as switch tiles toggle; components light up as a flat fill change (never a glow). Signature: the circuit and its scope trace change together in at least one scene of every episode.
- Colour-coding (fixed): n and electron dots amber, I violet, V teal, e and Q orange, t and R ink. Same colour in diagram, formula and readout.
- Motion: one thing moves at a time, formulas and lines draw on stroke by stroke, 0.6-0.8 s script pause after each derivation step, camera push-in max 1.15x once per scene, no particles, glows or 3D. Dots and scope scroll are the only constant-speed motion.
- Transitions: notebook page turn with the amber strip only for the statement, the exam segment and the formula card; elsewhere vis/rise.
- Sounds: only M.sfx(t,'whoosh',0.22) on page turns, 3 ticks (0.28) in the MCQ countdown (PUC.mcqSfx), 'correct' (0.32) on the answer. Nothing else.
- Cast: hook only (lead as listed per episode in the bible), then small face avatars with one-line reactions. The concept carries the video.
- Exam segment: "Where it is asked" chips light only exams verified from a cited past-paper analysis; if not verifiable, light none and state only the question type. MCQ is ORIGINAL (never copied), real JEE/board level, 4 options, answer and every solution step checked by hand and by running the arithmetic (python).
- Facts: Karnataka PUC Electronics textbook scope first, NCERT Physics for overlapping constants and laws. Never invent exam weightage, counts or statistics. Illustrative circuit values carry the ghost chip "EXAMPLE FIGURES". No brand or model names (no company, no chip part numbers).
- Length 4:00 to 6:00 of voice. 12-year-old language, exam substance underneath. Plain hyphens only.
- If you introduce a recurring fact (a constant value, a symbol colour, a notation), state it in your report so the lead adds it to the bible.
