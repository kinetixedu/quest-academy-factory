## Who it is for
Any B.E./B.Tech Mechanical student in India, whatever the university, plus GATE ME aspirants. The topic list was built from the AICTE model curriculum, VTU 2022, Anna R2021, AKTU, JNTUH R22, SPPU 2019, KTU, GTU and the official GATE 2026 ME syllabus (Engineering Mechanics is in GATE ME Section 2). Never mention exam weightage or marks.

## Subject rules (engineering mechanics)
- Every force is an arrow drawn to scale (state the scale or keep the ratio honest), with its magnitude and unit in IBM Plex Mono: N, kN, m, s, m/s, m/s², N·m, kg·m². SI units only. g = 9.81 m/s².
- Every worked number is computed in Python before it goes on screen; round sensibly (3 significant figures) and show the rounding.
- Motion is physically correct: a resultant points along the true vector sum, a body in equilibrium does not drift, a falling or sliding body accelerates (no linear fake motion), wheels roll without slipping unless the episode is about slipping, gears mesh at the true ratio.
- Diagrams are hand-built SVG (free body diagrams, vector triangles, trusses, beams). Illustrations only for the cast and the workshop.

## The running story (canon - keep it consistent)
**Salim Auto Works** - a small auto-rickshaw repair workshop in Peenya, Bengaluru. Over the series the team turns one old diesel auto-rickshaw into a working electric auto, nicknamed **Bijli**. Every mechanics idea solves the next real problem in the build.
- **Kiran** - 20, second-year mechanical student, Salim's nephew, helps in the workshop after college. Curious, sketches every problem in a notebook before touching a tool. Orange safety vest over a grey T-shirt.
- **Salim chacha** - 58, owner and master mechanic, 30 years fixing autos. Calm, trusts his hands, says "measure first, then lift". Grey beard, steel-grey shirt, orange cloth on his shoulder.
- **Priya** - 22, final-year electrical student and Kiran's cousin. Battery, motor and controller. Blunt, practical, numbers person. Glasses, long braid, grey kurta with an orange dupatta.
- **Raju** - 34, auto-rickshaw driver, the first customer and test driver. Wants lower running cost and a safe ride for his passengers. Moustache, grey uniform shirt.
- **Farida** - 45, runs the spare-parts shop next door. Supplies parts and watches the budget. Hijab, steel-grey salwar suit, a parts ledger.
- Growth arc, in order:
  1. Eps 1-9 Statics in the workshop: pushing and towing the old auto, removing the diesel engine with a chain hoist, parking on a slope, tyres, chocks, a ladder, the drive belt, lifting it on a screw jack.
  2. Eps 10-14 Structures and the build: a steel truss roof for the new charging shed, the battery placed for a safe centre of gravity, the wheel and motor rotor, the scissor lift.
  3. Eps 15-20 Dynamics on the road: Bijli's first test runs with Raju - acceleration, turns on a roundabout, braking, speed bumps and impacts, the spinning wheel. Ep 20 ends the series with Bijli carrying its first passengers.
- Never introduce real brands into the story (no real vehicle makers, battery or tool brands). Real scientists (Newton, Varignon, Lami, Stevin, D'Alembert, Coulomb, Archimedes) appear only as text or quote cards with verified facts and dates.
- Workplace examples ("At work today") are generic, with no brand names: a bridge builder, a crane operator, a car maker, a bicycle maker, a dam, an airline, a lift company, a railway.
- Illustrative numbers carry the ghost chip "EXAMPLE FIGURES". All arithmetic on screen is correct.
