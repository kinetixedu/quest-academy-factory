Quest Academy Factory

The internal publishing tool of Quest Academy, the education brand of MR Quest Technologies (Bengaluru, India). It produces short animated lessons for college students (MBA, BBA, BCom, BCA, BSc and Engineering) and publishes them to our own YouTube channel.

Watch Quest Academy on YouTube

Our own content only

Every video is created by our team. The tool uploads only to the Quest Academy channel, authorised by the channel owner.

Uses YouTube API Services

Uploads, thumbnails, English captions, playlists and publish schedules are handled through the YouTube Data API v3.

Internal tool

Not a public service: there is no sign-up and no third-party users. Used only by MR Quest Technologies staff.

How a video is published

  1. Our team writes, voices and animates the lesson; the factory renders final.mp4, the thumbnail and English captions.
  2. The video is uploaded to our channel as private with a scheduled publish time (videos.insert).
  3. The custom thumbnail and captions are attached (thumbnails.set, captions.insert).
  4. The video is added to its subject playlist and series playlist (playlistItems.insert).
  5. The video ID and publish time are recorded so nothing is uploaded twice. See the factory dashboard.
