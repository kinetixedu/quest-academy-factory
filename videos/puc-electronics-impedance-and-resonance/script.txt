T1|0.8|At resonance, the coil and the capacitor cancel. Only R is left.
D1|0.35|Now the formula for impedance, in small steps.
D2|0.7|In the resistor, voltage and current are in step. In the coil, voltage leads the current by ninety degrees.
D3|0.7|In the capacitor, voltage lags by ninety degrees. So the coil and capacitor voltages point in opposite directions.
D4|0.7|They subtract. The net push from them is I times X L minus X C.
D5|0.7|This is at right angles to I R. So we join them with Pythagoras.
D6|0.7|Z equals the square root of R squared, plus X L minus X C, squared.
D7|0.7|The phase angle, phi, follows from tan phi equals X L minus X C, over R.
D8|0.7|For resonance, set X L equal to X C. Two pi f L equals one over two pi f C.
D9|0.65|So the resonant frequency is f naught equals one over two pi, root L C. There, Z equals R, the smallest it can be.
