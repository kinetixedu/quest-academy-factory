# Ep 14 - Orthographic Projections - notes

## Facts (verified)
- Orthographic = parallel projection with the plane of projection perpendicular to the parallel lines of sight. Source: IIT Guwahati ME111 lecture 5, https://iitg.ac.in/rkbc/ME111/Lecture5%20Orthographic%20proj%20and%20proj%20of%20lines.pdf (front/top share width via vertical projectors, front/side share height).
- First angle: object lies between observer and plane of projection; third angle: planes lie between object and observer. Source: https://lecture-notes.tiu.edu.iq/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/PTR-111-Lecture-6-Orthographic-Projections.pdf
- First angle view placement: top view under the front view, left-side view to the right of the front view. Source: https://www.gdandtbasics.com/?p=6606
- India: SP 46 : 2003 notes on use: "As a basic requirement use of FIRST ANGLE METHOD only is to be followed for drawings prepared after 31 December 1991"; symbol of the method "shall be placed in a space provided for the purpose in the title block". Source: https://law.resource.org/pub/in/bis/S01/is.sp.46.2003.pdf
- Symbol is a cone (top and side view of a cone) in the title block. Source: https://www.gdandtbasics.com/?p=6783
- Gaspard Monge (9 May 1746 - 28 July 1818), French mathematician, commonly presented as the inventor of descriptive geometry; 1795 lectures published 1799 as "Geometrie descriptive". Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaspard_Monge

## Process to animate (M0-M8, F1-F4)
1. 3D model of three steps (rise 150, tread 300, width 1200).
2. Vertical glass plane behind (VP); projectors from each corner -> front view zigzag 900 x 450 draws on VP.
3. Glass floor under (HP); projectors drop -> top view rectangle 900 x 1200 with step lines.
4. Side glass (profile plane) -> left view (rectangle 1200 x 450 with step lines).
5. Glass box unfolds like a book: HP swings down below VP, side plane swings out to the right.
6. Flat sheet: front, top below, left view on right; thin alignment lines show shared length (front/top) and shared height (front/side). First angle cone symbol appears in the title block.
