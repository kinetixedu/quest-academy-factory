L4|0.4|Now push P off the line. The slope changes at once. Zero point eight. Not one half.

D1|0.4|Let us write that rule down, in small steps.
D2|0.75|Step one. The slope from P zero to P is y minus y zero, over x minus x zero.
D3|0.75|Step two. For P to be on the line, this slope must equal m.
D4|0.75|Step three. Multiply both sides by x minus x zero.
D5|0.75|Step four. y minus y zero equals m times, x minus x zero. This is the point slope form.
D6|0.65|For our line, y minus two equals one half times, x minus one.
