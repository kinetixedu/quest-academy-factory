D1|0.35|Now the method, one step at a time.
D2|0.75|Step one. Pick one body, and draw it alone.
D3|0.75|Step two. Draw every force on it. Weight first, then one for each thing touching it.
D4|0.75|Step three. Choose axes: x along the motion, y across it.

D6|0.75|Step five. Along x, the sum of the forces equals m a. So T minus f equals m a.
