D1|0.35|Now let's write it down, step by step.
D2|0.75|Step one. At speed v on a circle of radius r, the acceleration is v squared over r, toward the centre.
D3|0.75|Step two. Newton's second law. The net force equals mass times acceleration.
D4|0.75|Step three. So the net inward force, F c, is m v squared over r. With v equal to omega r, it is m omega squared r.
D5|0.75|Step four. Units. Kilograms times metres per second squared, which is newtons.
