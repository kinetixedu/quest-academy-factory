D1|0.35|Now the rule, step by step.
D2|0.75|Step one. f s max is proportional to N. So f s max equals mu s times N.
D3|0.75|Step two. In the same way, f k equals mu k times N.
D4|0.75|Step three. So mu s is f s max over N. Here, zero point five. And mu k is zero point four.
D5|0.75|Step four. Newtons divided by newtons. The units cancel, so mu has no unit.
D6|0.75|Step five. Starting is harder than sliding, so mu s is bigger than mu k.
T1|0.7|Remember this. Friction equals mu times N. And mu depends on the two surfaces.
