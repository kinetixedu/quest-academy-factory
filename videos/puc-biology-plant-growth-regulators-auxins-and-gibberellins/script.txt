D1|0.35|Now, meet the auxins.
D2|0.45|IAA and IBA are natural auxins, found in plants. NAA and two four D are synthetic, made by people.
D3|0.45|Auxins are made at the growing tips of stems and roots, and move to where they act.

G1|0.35|Now, the gibberellins. Their story starts with a rice disease.
