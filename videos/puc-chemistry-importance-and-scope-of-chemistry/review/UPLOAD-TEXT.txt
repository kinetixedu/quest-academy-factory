Food From Air? Chemistry Explained Simply | PUC Chemistry Class 11

DESCRIPTION
A farmer grows more rice, a doctor treats cancer, and a thin glass thread carries your video call. Three different stories, one science behind all of them. In this animated lesson for PUC and Class 11 Chemistry (NCERT Chapter 1, Some Basic Concepts of Chemistry) we see why chemistry matters and how far it reaches.

You will see what chemists ask about matter (what it is made of, how it is built, how it behaves and how it changes), and watch two hydrogen atoms share electrons with oxygen to make water. On the molecule bench, nitrogen from the air and hydrogen break their bonds and rearrange into ammonia, the starting point for fertilisers like urea. A live balance counter shows why N2 + H2 -> NH3 is not balanced, and we balance it step by step to N2 + 3H2 -> 2NH3. A worked example checks the masses on both sides (28 u + 6 u = 34 u), then we map where chemistry reaches: life saving drugs (cisplatin and taxol for cancer, AZT for AIDS), fertilisers and pesticides, new materials (superconducting ceramics, conducting polymers, optical fibres), industry, and safer replacements for CFCs. Then the exam trap about changing subscripts, an original NEET pattern question with a full solution, a recap card and three questions to check yourself.

Made for PUC (Karnataka), CBSE Class 11, NEET and JEE Main students.
