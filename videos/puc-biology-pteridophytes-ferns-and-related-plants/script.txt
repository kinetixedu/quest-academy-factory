R1|0.35|What happens to a spore? Watch it in five stages.
R2|0.75|Stage one. The spore lands on damp soil and grows into a prothallus: a tiny, flat, green plant that lives on its own.
R3|0.75|Stage two. The prothallus makes the sex organs: antheridia, the male ones, and archegonia, the female ones.
R4|0.75|Stage three. Male gametes, called antherozoids, swim through water to the mouth of the archegonium.
R5|0.75|Stage four. One fuses with the egg and makes a zygote.
R6|0.75|Stage five. The zygote grows into a new fern, the sporophyte, the big plant we see.
R7|0.65|The prothallus needs cool, damp shade, and fertilisation needs water. So pteridophytes can spread only in narrow regions.
