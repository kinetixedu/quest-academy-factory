TWO KINDS OF FORCE
Gravity checks only two things
where you start, and where you end
The path does not change its work
so gravity is a conservative force
Friction takes more every metre
its work depends on the path
Friction is a non-conservative force
START AND END · OR THE WHOLE PATH?
Conservative

Non-conservative
E.G. FRICTION
Work grows with every metre of the path
