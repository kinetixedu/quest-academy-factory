P1|0.35|Now the second puzzle. An ovary is built from units called carpels. Watch where the ovules end up.
P2|0.75|Marginal. One carpel folds like a pea pod. The placenta forms a ridge along one seam, and the ovules sit in two rows on it. Like pea.
P3|0.75|Axile. Several carpels join, and their walls meet in the middle. The placenta is on the central axis, and the ovary has many rooms. China rose, tomato and lemon.
P4|0.75|Parietal. The ovules grow on the inner wall of the ovary. It has one room, but in mustard and Argemone, a false wall splits it into two.
P5|0.75|Free central. There are no walls inside. The ovules sit on a central column. Dianthus and primrose.
P6|0.75|Basal. The placenta grows at the base of the ovary, and holds just one ovule. Sunflower and marigold.
