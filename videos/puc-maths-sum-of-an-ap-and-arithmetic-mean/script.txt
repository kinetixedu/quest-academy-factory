D1|0.4|Now any A P. Its sum of n terms is called S n. Its last term is called l.

D3|0.75|Step two. Write it backwards. l, then l minus d, down to a.
D4|0.75|Step three. Add the rows. One term goes up by d, the other goes down by d. So every column is a plus l.
