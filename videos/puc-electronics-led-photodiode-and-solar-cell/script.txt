L2|0.3|Electrons wait at the top. Holes, the empty seats, wait at the bottom.

L4|0.35|On the breadboard: a cell, a resistor and our diode.
L5|0.35|Forward bias means the plus side of the cell meets the p side.
L6|0.35|Current flows. Electrons meet holes at the junction, and the diode glows.

P1|0.3|Now the second job. A photodiode turns light into a signal.

P3|0.35|We connect it in reverse bias. In the dark, almost no current flows.
