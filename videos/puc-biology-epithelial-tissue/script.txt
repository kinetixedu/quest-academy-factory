L1|0.35|Now let's sort simple epithelium, step by step.

L3|0.75|Step two. Squamous cells are thin and flat, with uneven edges, like floor tiles. They form the walls of blood vessels and the air sacs of the lungs, where things must pass through easily.
L4|0.75|Step three. Cuboidal cells are cube-shaped. They line the ducts of glands and the tubes of the kidney, and help in secretion and absorption.
L5|0.75|Step four. Columnar cells are tall, with nuclei at the base. They line the stomach and the intestine.
