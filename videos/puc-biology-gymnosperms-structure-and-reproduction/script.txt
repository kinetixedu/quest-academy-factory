R1|0.35|How does a gymnosperm make a seed? Watch it in five stages.
R2|0.75|Stage one. Male cones make microspores. Each grows into a pollen grain, a male gametophyte made of only a few cells.
R3|0.75|Stage two. In the ovule, a megaspore mother cell divides by meiosis into four megaspores. One grows into the female gametophyte.
R4|0.75|Stage three. Air currents carry the pollen grains to the opening of the ovules. This is pollination.
R5|0.75|Stage four. A pollen tube grows towards the archegonia and releases the male gametes near them. One fuses with the egg.
R6|0.75|Stage five. The zygote grows into an embryo, and the ovule becomes a seed. No ovary wall, so no fruit. The seed stays naked.
