# Ep 09 - Friction and Its Applications - notes

## Facts used
- Guillaume Amontons published (rediscovered) the laws of friction in 1699; verified by Charles-Augustin de Coulomb in 1781. Friction proportional to load, independent of apparent contact area. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guillaume_Amontons
- Ramp 1:12: Harmonised Guidelines and Standards for Universal Accessibility in India 2021 (CPWD, MoHUA, Dec 2021) recommends 1:12 for a 6 m length; 1:12 is the maximum, gentler for longer runs. https://www.gktoday.in/guidelines-for-universal-accessibility-in-india-2021/ and https://www.studiomatrx.org/students/building-codes/accessibility-and-compliance . Script does not name the guideline (could go on screen as a small source line if lead wants).
- Ladder 4-to-1 rule (base out one quarter of working length, about 75 deg): US OSHA 29 CFR 1926.1053(b)(5)(i), https://wcsafety.com/blogs/reference/osha-ladder-requirements . Script uses it as generic site practice, no code named.
- Angle of repose: tan(phi) = mu (standard statics).

## Numbers
- Brick W = 20 N; slides at P = 10 N -> mu = 10/20 = 0.5 (EXAMPLE FIGURES).
- Angle of repose = atan(0.5) = 26.57 deg ("about twenty six and a half").
- Dusty mu = 0.3 (EXAMPLE) -> atan(0.3) = 16.70 deg ("under seventeen").
- Ramp 1:12 -> atan(1/12) = 4.76 deg ("under five degrees"). Verified gradient, not example.
