D1|0.35|Let's write this down, one step at a time.
D2|0.75|Step one. In translation, every point has the same displacement in the same time. So, the same velocity.
D3|0.75|Step two. In rotation about a fixed axis, every point turns through the same angle theta, in the same time t.
D4|0.75|Step three. A point at distance r from the axis moves along an arc. s equals r theta, with theta in radians.
D5|0.75|Step four. Its speed is s over t, or r theta over t. Double r, and the speed doubles.
D6|0.6|A point on the axis has r equal to zero. It does not move.
T1|0.7|Remember this. In translation, every point moves the same way. In rotation, every point turns through the same angle.
