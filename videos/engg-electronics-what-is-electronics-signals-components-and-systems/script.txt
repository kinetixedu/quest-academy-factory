D1|0.3|So what is electronics?
D2|0.35|Electricity is moving charge. It can light a bulb or run a motor.
D3|0.35|Electronics uses that current to carry information and make decisions.
D4|0.65|In simple words: electrical power does the work. Electronics decides when and how.
G1|0.35|The information travels as a signal. A signal is a quantity that changes to carry a message.
G2|0.35|An analog signal changes smoothly, like the soil getting drier hour by hour.
G3|0.65|A digital signal has only two levels, on and off. We write them as one and zero.
C1|0.35|Signals flow through components.
C2|0.35|Passive components, like resistors and capacitors, cannot add energy. They resist, store or guide it.
C3|0.65|Active components, like transistors and chips, can control the current and make a signal bigger.
Y0|0.35|Put components together for a purpose, and you get a system. Every system does three things.
Y1|0.3|Sense. A sensor turns the real world into a signal.
Y2|0.3|Decide. A circuit compares the signal with a rule.
Y3|0.65|Act. An output, like a pump or a light, does the work.
