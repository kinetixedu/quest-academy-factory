P1|0.35|Now the prion. To understand it, we need to know how proteins work. Step by step.
P2|0.75|Step one. A protein is a long chain that folds into an exact shape. Its shape decides its job.
P3|0.75|Step two. A prion is a protein that has folded the wrong way. An abnormally folded protein.
P4|0.75|Step three. When a prion meets a normal protein of the same kind, it makes that one fold wrongly too.
P5|0.75|Step four. Now there are two prions, then four, then many. It is a chain reaction.
P6|0.75|Step five. The wrongly folded proteins pile up and damage the nerve cells. So prion diseases are diseases of the nervous system.
