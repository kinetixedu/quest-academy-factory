# Ep 16 - Building Drawing - Plan, Elevation and Section - notes

## Facts (verified)
- IS 962 : 1989, "Code of practice for architectural and building drawings" (BIS). Sources: https://services.bis.gov.in/php/BIS_2.0/bisconnect/knowyourstandards/Indian_standards/isdetails/MTcwMjc= ; https://infralens.in/code/IS-962-1989 (title; building plans at 1:50 or 1:100, site plans 1:500 or 1:1000). Script names only number, year and title.
- Plan = horizontal cut through the building seen from above, cut at about 4 ft (1.2 m) above the floor, i.e. a little above window sill level so windows and doors show. Sources: https://blog.nobledesktop.com/learn/blueprint-reading/understanding-floor-plans-elevations-sections-wall-sections-and-details-in-construction-drawings ("about 4 feet 6 inches above the finished floor"); https://openlab.citytech.cuny.edu/christo-arch1101-fall2022/files/2022/09/Architectural-Drawings_Plan-Section-Elevation.pdf ("commonly about 4 feet"). Script says "a little above the window sills" (no number voiced).
- Elevation = one outside face seen straight on; section = vertical slice through the building. Same sources.
- Cut members drawn with thick lines, things seen beyond with thin lines; section line on the plan (A-A with arrows giving view direction) - standard drawing practice (SP 46 : 2003 section conventions, as used in Ep 13 notes).
- Hyatt Regency walkway collapse: 17 July 1981, Kansas City, Missouri; 114 dead, 200+ injured; the hanger rod detail was changed from one continuous rod to two rods, doubling the load on the fourth-floor connection (design load 20.3 to 40.7 kips). Source: https://onlineethics.virginia.edu/cases/hyatt-regency-walkway-collapse (no brand or company named in script).

## Process to animate (M0-M8)
1. Isometric of one classroom (7.5 m x 6 m, door, three windows, plinth, slab, parapet) on the blueprint sheet.
2. A translucent horizontal plane slides in at sill-plus height (chip "CUT ABOVE SILL"), the top half lifts off and floats away.
3. Camera tilts to look straight down; walls become thick outlines, door swing arc and window gaps draw on; dims 7500 and 6000 type in; chip "SCALE 1:100".
4. Observer icon walks round to the front; the front face flattens into the elevation: windows, door, plinth band, parapet line.
5. Back on the plan: cutting line A-A draws across through a window, with view arrows.
6. A vertical plane drops along A-A; the room splits; camera turns to look in from the side.
7. Section builds bottom up: footing (hatched), plinth, floor, sill, window, lintel, slab, parapet; cut parts thick, beyond parts thin; level tags type on (+0.00 FFL, sill +0.90, lintel +2.10, slab +3.30) - EXAMPLE FIGURES.
8. All three sheets sit side by side, linked by thin projector lines (plan below elevation, section to the side).
