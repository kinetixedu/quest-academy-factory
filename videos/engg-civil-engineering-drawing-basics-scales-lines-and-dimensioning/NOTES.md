# Ep 13 - Engineering Drawing Basics - Scales, Lines and Dimensioning - notes

## Facts (verified)
- SP 46 : 2003, "Engineering Drawing Practice for Schools and Colleges", Bureau of Indian Standards. Sources: https://www.bis.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/BISPresentationonEnggDrawing.pdf and full text https://law.resource.org/pub/in/bis/S01/is.sp.46.2003.pdf
- SP 46 section 5.4 recommended scales: reduction 1:2, 1:5, 1:10, 1:20, 1:50, 1:100, 1:200, 1:500, 1:1000, 1:2000, 1:5000, 1:10000; full size 1:1; enlargement 2:1 to 50:1. Designation "SCALE 1:X". (Script X6: "from one to two, all the way to one to ten thousand".)
- SP 46 line types: visible outlines = continuous wide (thick) line; hidden outlines = dashed narrow line; centre lines = long-dashed dotted narrow line; dimension lines and extension lines = continuous narrow line. Tables 6.5 and 6.8.
- SP 46 lettering nominal heights 1.8, 2.5, 3.5, 5, 7, 10, 14, 20 mm (section 7.4.3) - not voiced in final cut, may show on screen.
- Table headings in SP 46 "All dimensions in millimetres"; practice of dimensioning in mm and writing true size is standard. Section 12 (dimensioning) not readable in the online text, so script states only generic rules (thin extension + dimension lines, arrowheads, true size).
- "Do not scale" note: common drawing practice; presented as Farhan's note, not as a code clause.

## Process to animate (M0-M8, E1-E3)
1. Blank sheet with border and title block outline.
2. Scale chip "SCALE 1:100"; 7500 mm room -> divide by 100 -> 75 mm rectangle draws; 6000 -> 60 mm.
3. Walls redraw in thick continuous lines; a dashed beam line across; chain-line centre line.
4. Extension lines, dimension line with arrowheads draw on; text "7500" (true size) types in; a crossed-out "75" paper size.
5. Title block fills: drawing name, scale, drawn by Farhan, date.
6. Zoom to door: chip changes to 1:50; 3 m wall bar grows 30 mm -> 60 mm side by side.
