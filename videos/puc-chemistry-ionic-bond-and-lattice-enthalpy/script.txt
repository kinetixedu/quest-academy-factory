D1|0.35|Now the definition, step by step.
D2|0.75|Step one. Lattice enthalpy is the energy needed to completely separate one mole of a solid ionic compound into its gaseous ions.
D3|0.75|Step two. For sodium chloride, it is seven hundred and eighty eight kilojoules per mole. That much energy pulls the crystal fully apart.
D4|0.75|Step three. Bigger charges pull harder. Ions with charges of two pull far more strongly than ions with charges of one.
D5|0.75|Step four. Smaller ions sit closer, and closer charges pull harder too. Small size and big charge mean a big lattice enthalpy.
T1|0.7|Remember this. The lattice pays for making the ions. Strong lattice, stable ionic solid.
