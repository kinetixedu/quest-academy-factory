S1|0.35|Now a gas in a cylinder, with a piston on top, on our molecule bench.

S4|0.4|Way two: heat. Put a flame under the cylinder. Heat flows in, and again the molecules speed up.

S6|0.65|So energy crosses the boundary in only two ways. As heat, q, and as work, w.
V1|0.35|Now the signs. Stand inside the system, and watch your bank.
V2|0.4|Heat flowing in, from the surroundings, is plus. Heat flowing out is minus.
V3|0.4|Work done on the system is plus. Work done by the system is minus.
V4|0.4|Just like money. Money in is plus, money out is minus.
V5|0.65|Add them up. Delta U equals q plus w. This is the First Law of Thermodynamics.
B1|0.35|In words, energy can neither be made nor destroyed. It only changes form.

D1|0.35|How big is the work, when a gas expands? Step by step.
D2|0.75|The outside pressure is p ex. It pushes on the piston from outside.
D3|0.75|The gas grows from volume V i to volume V f. The change is delta V.
D4|0.75|Work equals minus p ex times delta V.

D6|0.65|If a gas expands into empty space, a vacuum, nothing pushes back. p ex is zero, so the work is zero. This is free expansion.
