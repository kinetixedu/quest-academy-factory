# Ep 15 - Isometric Projections - notes

## Facts (verified)
- Isometric axes meet at 120 degrees to each other; the two receding axes are at 30 degrees to the horizontal. Isometric scale: projected length = cos 35 deg 16 min = 0.8165 x true length. Source: NPTEL Module 4, https://archive.nptel.ac.in/content/storage2/courses/112103019/module4/lec38/3.html ; also https://www.iitg.ac.in/rkbc/ME111/Lecture15%20Isometric%20projection-pkghosh.pdf
- Isometric drawing (true lengths) vs isometric projection (isometric scale): drawing is 1 / 0.8165 = 1.2247 times bigger, about 22 % larger. Standard textbook distinction.
- William Farish (1759-1837), Cambridge professor, published "On Isometrical Perspective" in 1822, Transactions of the Cambridge Philosophical Society vol. 1. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Farish_(chemist)
- Circles appear as ellipses in isometric; lines not parallel to the axes are not true length (dropped from final script, may show on screen).
