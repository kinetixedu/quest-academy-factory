D1|0.35|Now the formula, one step at a time.
D2|0.75|Step one. A force F pushes a body from rest through a distance d. The work done is W equals F d.
D3|0.75|Step two. Newton's second law. F equals m a.
D4|0.75|Step three. Starting from rest, v squared equals two a d. So d equals v squared over two a.
D5|0.75|Step four. Put them together. W equals m a, times v squared over two a. The a cancels. W equals half m v squared.
D6|0.75|Step five. This work is stored in the motion. So kinetic energy K equals half m v squared, in joules.
D7|0.75|Step six. Momentum p equals m v. So K can also be written as p squared over two m.
T1|0.7|Remember this. Kinetic energy is half m v squared. Double the speed, and you get four times the energy.
