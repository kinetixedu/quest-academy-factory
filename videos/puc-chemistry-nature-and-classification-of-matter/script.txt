P1|0.35|First, what is matter?
P2|0.35|Matter is anything that has mass and takes up space. It comes in three states.
P3|0.4|A solid has a fixed shape and a fixed volume. Its particles sit close together and only shake in place.
P4|0.4|A liquid has a fixed volume, but it takes the shape of its container. Its particles slide past each other.
P5|0.4|A gas has neither. Its particles fly apart and fill any space they get.
P6|0.65|Change the temperature or the pressure, and one state turns into another. Ice melts into water, and water boils into vapour.
U1|0.35|Now let's sort matter into boxes. Every sample is either a mixture or a pure substance.
U2|0.4|A mixture holds two or more substances, in any ratio.
U3|0.4|If it is the same all through, like sugar in water, it is homogeneous. Homo means same.
U4|0.4|If its parts are not the same everywhere, like grains of salt and sugar mixed together, it is heterogeneous.
U5|0.4|A pure substance has only one kind of particle. An element has only one kind of atom, like copper or oxygen.
U6|0.65|A compound has atoms of different elements, joined in a fixed ratio, like water or carbon dioxide.
S1|0.35|Let's look inside air on our molecule bench.
