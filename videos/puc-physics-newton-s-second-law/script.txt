D1|0.35|Now let's write the rule, one step at a time.
D2|0.75|Step one. Keep the mass fixed. a is proportional to F.
D3|0.75|Step two. Keep the force fixed. a is proportional to one over m.
D4|0.75|Step three. Together, a is proportional to F over m. So F equals k, m, a, with k a constant.
D5|0.75|Step four. We pick the unit of force so that k is exactly one. So F equals m a.
