D1|0.35|Now, how stable are these pieces? Step by step.
D2|0.75|Step one. A methyl group, C H three, gently pushes electrons towards the carbon it is joined to.
D3|0.75|Step two. A carbocation is short of electrons. Each methyl group around it feeds it a little, so more methyl groups make it more stable.
