S7|0.35|Slow-mo replay. Freeze.
S8|0.4|Every point gets the forward velocity of the centre, v. Plus a spin velocity, R omega, along the rim.
S9|0.4|At the top, both point forward. v plus v. Two v.
S10|0.4|At the bottom, they point opposite ways. v minus v. Zero. So the bottom spokes look sharp.
S11|0.6|At the front, they are at right angles. Root two times v.
R1|0.35|But why must v equal R omega? Link by link.
R2|0.4|In one full turn, every point of the rim touches the road once.
R3|0.4|So in one turn, the centre moves forward by one rim length. Two pi R.
R4|0.6|That takes one turn time, T. So v is two pi R over T, which is R omega.
D1|0.35|Now the rules, one step at a time.
D2|0.75|Step one. No slipping: v of the centre equals R omega.
D3|0.75|Step two. Any point moves with v of the centre, plus its own spin velocity.
D4|0.75|Step three. At the top: v plus R omega. Two v.
D5|0.75|Step four. At the bottom: v minus R omega. Zero. The contact point is at rest.
D6|0.75|Step five. At each instant, the wheel turns about the contact point. Speed is omega times the distance from it.
T1|0.7|Remember this. In rolling without slipping, the bottom point is at rest, and the top moves at twice the speed of the centre.
K1|0.4|Side by side. Rolling: the bottom point is at rest, so the tyre grips the road.
K2|0.6|Skidding, brakes locked: the wheel does not turn. The bottom point slides at v, and scrapes.

K4|0.65|No! The centre stays still, so the bottom point moves backward at R omega. Only true rolling makes it zero.
