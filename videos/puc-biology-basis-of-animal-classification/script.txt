L1|0.35|Here are the six questions.
L2|0.75|One. Level of organisation. Do the cells work loosely, or form tissues, organs and organ systems?
L3|0.75|Two. Symmetry. Does a cut give two matching halves, and in how many directions?
L4|0.75|Three. Germ layers. Does the embryo, the animal at its earliest stage, build two layers or three?
L5|0.75|Four. Coelom. Is there a body cavity between the gut and the body wall, lined by mesoderm, the middle germ layer?
L6|0.75|Five. Segmentation. Is the body built from repeated segments, like an earthworm?
L7|0.75|Six. Notochord. Does the embryo form a stiff rod along its back? It splits chordates from non-chordates.
L8|0.65|Biologists also compare systems. Is the gut a bag with one opening, or a tube with two?
