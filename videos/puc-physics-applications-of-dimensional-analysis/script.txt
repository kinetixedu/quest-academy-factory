D1|0.35|Let's build the formula, one step at a time.
D2|0.75|Step one. Guess T equals k, times m to the x, l to the y, g to the z. k is a pure number.
D3|0.75|Step two. Dimensions. T on the left. M to the x, L to the y, and L T to the minus two, to the z.
D4|0.75|Step three. Match the powers of M. There is no M on the left, so x is zero.
D5|0.75|Step four. Match the powers of T. One equals minus two z. So z is minus one half.
D6|0.75|Step five. Match the powers of L. Zero equals y plus z. So y is plus one half.
D7|0.7|So T equals k times the square root of l over g. We found it without a single experiment.
R1|0.35|Now read what this formula is telling us. Follow the links.
R2|0.4|x is zero, so mass has no power. So the mass of the bob does not matter.
R3|0.4|y is one half, so T grows with the square root of the length.
R4|0.65|z is minus one half. So stronger gravity means a faster swing.

C1|0.35|Now the second use. Converting units.

C3|0.4|Energy has dimensions M L squared T to the minus two.
C4|0.75|So the number changes by the mass ratio, times the length ratio squared, times the time ratio to the minus two.
