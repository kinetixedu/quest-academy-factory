# Ep 18 - Lime, Timber, Glass and Plastics - notes

## Facts (verified)
- Fibre saturation point 28-30 % moisture content; above it free water leaves cell cavities with no shrinkage; below it bound water leaves the cell walls and the wood shrinks. Air drying typically reaches 15-20 %; kilns (enclosed, elevated temperature, controlled humidity) go lower. Source: Purdue Extension FNR-37, https://extension.purdue.edu/extmedia/FNR/FNR-37.html
- IS 287 : 1993 "Permissible Moisture Content for Timber Used for Different Purposes - Recommendations": doors and windows (50 mm and thicker) max 10 / 12 / 14 / 16 % for Zones I-IV (by average annual relative humidity). Source: https://www.infralens.in/code/IS-287-1993 . Superseded by IS 287 : 2022 (values not read) and Hosahalli's zone not confirmed, so the script names no code and the 12 % target is an EXAMPLE FIGURE.
- Lime cycle: limestone burnt at about 900 C -> quicklime (CaO); + water (slaking) -> calcium hydroxide + heat; on the wall it slowly absorbs CO2 from the air and returns to calcium carbonate. Source: https://lime.org.uk/knowledge-base/introduction-to-lime-lime-and-its-production
- Lime vs cement on old walls: lime is softer, more flexible and more breathable; cement mortar/render is hard, brittle, traps moisture and damages old masonry. Source: Salford City Council conservation guidance, https://www.salford.gov.uk/planning-building-and-regeneration/conservation-and-listed-buildings/specialist-conservation-advice/traditional-materials-and-construction-methods/lime
- Soda-lime glass: about 70 % silica, 15 % soda, 9 % lime. Source: Britannica, https://www.britannica.com/print/article/551995
- Toughened (tempered) glass: heated to about 620 C then air-quenched; about four times stronger than annealed glass; breaks into small granular chunks. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tempered_glass
- India banned identified single-use plastic items from 1 July 2022 (Plastic Waste Management Amendment Rules, 2021). Source: PIB, https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2022/jul/doc20227169001.pdf (not voiced; available for screen if X3 needs it).

## Process to animate (M0-M7, B0-B3)
1. Door frame plank close-up; moisture meter clips on, reads 60 %.
2. Wood cell diagram: hollow cell (cavity) full of blue free water, cell wall shaded blue too.
3. Stack with spacer sticks; air arrows flow; meter counts down 60 -> 45 -> 30; cavity water drains, plank width does NOT change (dim line stays).
4. Line at 30 % ("FIBRE SATURATION POINT"); below it the wall water drains and the plank width shrinks (dim line ticks down); meter 30 -> 20, chip "AIR DRY 15-20 %".
5. Plank into kiln box (heat waves, humidity gauge); meter 20 -> 12 %; chip "READY".
6. Side panel: unseasoned frame racks out of square (door gap opens) vs seasoned frame stays true.
7. Lime cycle wheel: limestone -> kiln (~900 C) -> quicklime -> + water (steam puffs, thermometer up) -> slaked lime -> on wall, CO2 molecules drift in -> limestone again (full circle).
8. Glass pie: ~70 % silica, 15 % soda, 9 % lime, rest other; PVC pipe icon with a rust cross-out.
