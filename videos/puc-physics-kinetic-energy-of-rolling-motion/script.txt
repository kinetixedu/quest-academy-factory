D1|0.35|Now the rule, one step at a time.
D2|0.75|Step one. Total energy is one half m v squared, plus one half I omega squared.
D3|0.75|Step two. Put omega equal to v over R, and I equal to m k squared. K equals one half m v squared, times one plus k squared over R squared.
D4|0.75|Step three. The bottom point does not slip, so friction does no work. So m g h equals that energy.
D5|0.75|Step four. v equals root of two g h, over one plus k squared over R squared. The mass cancels.
D6|0.75|Step five. For a ring, k squared over R squared is one. For a disc, a half. For a ball, two fifths. The smallest value wins.
