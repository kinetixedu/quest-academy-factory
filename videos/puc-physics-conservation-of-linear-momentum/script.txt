D1|0.35|Now let's prove it, one step at a time.
D2|0.75|Step one. In the crash, B pushes A with a force F A B. A pushes B with F B A.
D3|0.75|Step two. By the third law, F A B equals minus F B A.
D4|0.75|Step three. Both act for the same time, delta t. Force times time is the change in momentum.
D5|0.75|Step four. Add the two changes. The forces cancel, so delta p A plus delta p B is zero.
D6|0.75|Step five. So the total after equals the total before. p A plus p B stays constant.
