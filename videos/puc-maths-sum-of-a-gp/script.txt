D1|0.4|Why does this work? Here is the trick for any G P.
D2|0.75|Step one. Write the sum. S n is a, plus a r, plus a r squared, up to a r to the power n minus one.
D3|0.75|Step two. Multiply every term by r. r S n is a r, plus a r squared, up to a r to the power n.
D4|0.75|Step three. Line them up. Almost every term is in both rows. Subtract, and they cancel in pairs.
D5|0.75|Only two terms survive. r S n minus S n is a r to the power n, minus a.
D6|0.75|Step four. Take out S n. S n times r minus one is a times r to the power n, minus one.
D7|0.75|Step five. Divide by r minus one. S n is a times r to the power n minus one, over r minus one.
D8|0.65|Check the chessboard. a is one and r is two. We get two to the power sixty four, minus one. It matches.
C1|0.4|Side by side. When r is bigger than one, use r to the power n minus one, over r minus one. Top and bottom stay positive.
C2|0.6|When r is smaller than one, use one minus r to the power n, over one minus r. Same value, just tidier.
