D2|0.75|Step one. Angular velocity, omega, is angular displacement over time. Omega equals delta theta over delta t, in radians per second.
D3|0.75|Step two. For a point at radius r, the arc it covers is s equals r theta, with theta in radians.
D4|0.75|Step three. Divide both sides by the time. s over t is the speed v, and theta over t is omega.
D5|0.75|So v equals r omega. The same omega, a bigger radius, a bigger speed.
