D1|0.35|Let's see why force times speed gives power, step by step.
D2|0.75|Step one. Average power is the total work, divided by the total time. P equals W over t.

D5|0.75|Step four. d r by d t is the velocity. So P equals F dot v, which is F v cos theta.
