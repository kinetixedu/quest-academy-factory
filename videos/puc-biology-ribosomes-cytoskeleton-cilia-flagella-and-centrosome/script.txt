D1|0.35|First, the protein builders.
D2|0.45|Ribosomes are tiny grains. George Palade first saw them as dense particles under the electron microscope, in 1953.
D3|0.45|They are made of RNA and proteins, and they have no membrane around them. Their job is to build proteins.
D4|0.45|Every ribosome has two parts, called subunits. In eukaryotic cells, ribosomes are 80S, made of a 60S and a 40S subunit. In prokaryotic cells, they are 70S, made of a 50S and a 30S subunit.

Z1|0.35|Now let's zoom into the cell's skeleton, one level at a time.
