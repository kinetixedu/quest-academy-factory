D1|0.35|Let's prove the link, one step at a time.
D2|0.75|Step one. For a particle, L equals r cross p.
D3|0.75|Step two. Find how it changes. d L by d t equals d r by d t cross p, plus r cross d p by d t.
D4|0.75|Step three. d r by d t is the velocity v, and p is m v. They point the same way, so v cross p is zero.
D5|0.75|Step four. d p by d t is the force F. So d L by d t equals r cross F, which is the torque.
D6|0.75|Step five. For a body made of many particles, the inside torques cancel in pairs. Only outside torques change the total L.
T1|0.7|Remember this. Torque is the rate of change of angular momentum.
