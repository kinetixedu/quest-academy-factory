# Ep 19 - Steel and Green Building Materials - notes (last of Year 1)

## Facts (verified)
- IS 1786 : 2008 "High Strength Deformed Steel Bars and Wires for Concrete Reinforcement - Specification" (Fourth Revision), still current on BIS with amendments. Sources: https://services.bis.gov.in/php/BIS_2.0/bisconnect/knowyourstandards/Indian_standards/isdetails/ODE5NQ== ; text https://law.resource.org/pub/in/bis/S03/is.1786.2008.pdf
- Table 3 (as amended): Fe 500D min 0.2 % proof / yield stress 500 N/mm2, min tensile strength 565 N/mm2 (TS/YS >= 1.10), min elongation 16.0 %; Fe 500 is 500 / 545 / 12.0 %; Fe 415 is 415 / 485 / 14.5 %. Same source; also https://infralens.in/code/IS-1786-2008/clause/grades
- TMT (quench and self-temper) process: water quench right after the last mill stand makes a hard martensite skin; heat from the hot core then tempers that skin; air cooling on the bed turns the core into ductile ferrite-pearlite. Source: https://www.ispatguru.com/quenched-and-tempered-reinforcement-bars-quenched-and-tempered-reinforcement-bars/
- Tensile test stages (elastic, yield, strain hardening, ultimate, necking, fracture). Source: IIT Kanpur virtual lab, https://mec-met-iitk.vlabs.ac.in/exp/tensile-test-strain/theory.html
- Cement: "more than 4 billion tonnes of cement are produced, accounting for around 8 per cent of global CO2 emissions". Chatham House explainer, 12 June 2018, https://www.chathamhouse.org/2018/06/why-cement-major-contributor-climate-change
- Fly ash: MoEF notification S.O. 763(E), 14 September 1999, required brick makers within 50 km of coal / lignite thermal power plants to mix at least 25 % ash. Source: https://bspcb.bihar.gov.in/MoEF&CC_1999.pdf (not voiced; script only says fly ash bricks reuse ash from coal power plants). Fly ash-lime bricks: IS 12894 : 2002 (https://infralens.in/code/IS-12894-2002) - not voiced.
- AAC: IS 2185 (Part 3) : 1984 covers autoclaved cellular concrete blocks with density up to 1000 kg/m3 (https://alephindia.in/isi-product/concrete-masonry-units-part-three-autoclaved-cellular-aerated-concrete-blocks.php); typical AAC 451-650 kg/m3 vs clay brick ~1600-1700 kg/m3 (https://www.bricknbolt.com/blogs-and-articles/construction-guide/aac-blocks-specifications). Not voiced as numbers.
- GRIHA (by TERI) adopted by MNRE in 2007 as India's National Rating System for Green Buildings. Source: https://www.grihaindia.org/

## Process to animate (M0-M8)
1. Red-hot bar leaves the last mill stand (glow gradient).
2. Water spray box: outer ring of the cross-section turns dark grey (chip "HARD SKIN").
3. Heat arrows flow from the orange core outward; skin lightens slightly (chip "SELF-TEMPERED").
4. Cooling bed: core turns a soft grey (chip "SOFT, BENDY CORE"); cross-section shows ring + core.
5. UTM: 12 mm bar gripped; load counter and live stress-strain curve draw together.
6. Elastic: small stretch, release, bar springs back (curve retraces).
7. Yield at 56.5 kN (chip "YIELD 500 N/mm2"); stretch grows and stays; curve flattens then rises.
8. Load passes 64 kN (chip "TS >= 565 N/mm2"); neck forms in one spot; snap; gauge length shows +16 % or more (200 mm -> 232 mm).
9. Side by side: brittle bar snaps with no stretch vs ductile bar (warning bend) - for T1.

## Numbers (EXAMPLE FIGURES: test bar results; code minimums verified)
- 12 mm bar area = pi x 12^2 / 4 = 113.1 mm2.
- Yield at 500 N/mm2: 113.1 x 500 = 56,549 N = 56.5 kN (voiced "fifty six and a half").
- Min tensile 565 N/mm2: 113.1 x 565 = 63,900 N = 63.9 kN -> script "past sixty four" (a passing bar exceeds the minimum; EXAMPLE).
- Elongation 16 % on a 200 mm gauge length (EXAMPLE gauge) = 32 mm -> 232 mm.
