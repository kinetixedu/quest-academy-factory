C1|0.35|A lichen is one kind of relationship between two species. Let's compare three kinds side by side.
C2|0.45|Mutualism: both partners gain. The lichen is one example. Another is mycorrhiza: a fungus on plant roots absorbs phosphorus from the soil for the plant, and the plant feeds the fungus.
