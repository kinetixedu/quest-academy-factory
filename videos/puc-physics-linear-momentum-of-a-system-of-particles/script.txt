D1|0.35|Now let's write the law, one step at a time.
D2|0.75|Step one. Add every momentum as vectors. P equals p one, plus p two, and so on, up to p n.
D3|0.75|Step two. Each p is m times v. The sum of all the m v is M V: total mass times the velocity of the centre of mass.
D4|0.75|Step three. How fast P changes each second, d P by d t, equals the sum of all the forces.
D5|0.75|Inside forces come in equal, opposite pairs, so they add to zero. Only the outside force is left.
D6|0.6|So d P by d t equals F external. With no outside force, P stays constant, and the centre of mass keeps a steady velocity.
T1|0.7|Remember this. Only an outside force can change the total momentum of a system.
