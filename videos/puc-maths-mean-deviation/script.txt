D1|0.4|Now the rule, for any list of numbers.
D2|0.75|Step one. Find the centre. The mean, x bar, is the sum of all the values, divided by how many there are.
D3|0.75|Step two. For each value, find its deviation, x minus x bar. That is how far it is from the centre, with a plus or a minus sign.
D4|0.75|Step three. Here is a problem. Add the deviations, and the pluses and minuses always cancel to zero.
D5|0.75|Step four. So we ignore the sign. These bars mean absolute value: the distance, which is never negative.
D6|0.75|Step five. Add the distances, and divide by n. That is the mean deviation about the mean.
D7|0.65|We can also measure from the median, the middle value. Then we write x minus M inside the bars.
C1|0.4|Side by side. Take two, four, six, eight and thirty. The mean is ten. Measured from ten, the mean deviation is eight.
C2|0.6|The median, the middle value, is six. Measured from six, the mean deviation is six point four.
