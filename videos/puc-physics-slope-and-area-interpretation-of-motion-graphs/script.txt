D4|0.75|Step three. The area under a velocity time graph is velocity times time. That is displacement.
D5|0.75|Step four. The area under an acceleration time graph is acceleration times time. That is the change in velocity.

R5|0.65|Add up all the strips, and you add up all the little distances. Total area, total displacement.
