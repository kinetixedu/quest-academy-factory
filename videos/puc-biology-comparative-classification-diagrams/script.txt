D1|0.35|The key asks only four questions, always in the same order.
D2|0.45|One. How are the cells organised? Two. What is the body symmetry?
D3|0.65|Three. Is there a body cavity, and what kind? Four. Is there a notochord? Each answer sends the animal down one branch.
B1|0.35|Let's build the key, branch by branch.
B2|0.75|Step one. Cells only loosely working together, at the cellular level? That is Porifera, the sponges. Everyone else has tissues, organs or organ systems.
B3|0.75|Step two. Radial symmetry, with two germ layers? Those are Coelenterata, also called Cnidaria, and Ctenophora.
B4|0.75|Step three. Bilateral symmetry, with three germ layers. Now check the body cavity. No cavity at all: Platyhelminthes, the flatworms.
B5|0.75|A false cavity, not lined by mesoderm: Aschelminthes, the roundworms.
B6|0.75|A true coelom: Annelida, Arthropoda, Mollusca, Echinodermata, Hemichordata and Chordata.
B7|0.65|Step four. A notochord? Only Chordata. Then jaws split the vertebrates, and fins or limbs split the jawed ones into fishes and four classes of tetrapods.
R1|0.35|Now watch the key work. Drop an earthworm in at the top.
R2|0.45|Organ system level, so it goes past the sponges. Bilateral, three germ layers.
R3|0.45|It has a true coelom. No notochord. It lands on Annelida.
R4|0.65|Now drop a crow. Coelomate, notochord, jaws, limbs, feathers. It lands on Aves.
K1|0.35|Two radial animals that sit on different branches. A jellyfish and a starfish.
K2|0.45|The jellyfish has two germ layers. The starfish has three.
K3|0.45|The jellyfish has no coelom. The starfish has a true coelom.
K4|0.65|And only the adult starfish is radial. Its larva is bilateral. So the key places it with the coelomates.
K5|3.2|Your turn. A leech has a true coelom, and its body is made of rings. Which phylum? Pause and decide.
K6|0.65|Annelida! A true coelom, bilateral, no notochord, and a body divided into segments. The leech is Hirudinaria in NCERT.
T1|0.6|Remember this. Cells, symmetry, cavity, notochord. Four questions, in that order, sort every animal.
