# Ep 22 - Sources of Energy - Thermal, Hydro, Nuclear, Solar, Wind

Suggested YouTube title (67 chars): `Power Sources Explained Simply | Basic Electrical Engineering Ep 22`

## Quiz
Q: Which source makes electricity without spinning any turbine?
- A) Coal
- B) Nuclear
- C) Hydro
- D) Solar (correct)
Correct: D. Takeaway strip: "Solar cells make DC directly - no moving parts."

## Recap rows
- Thermal, nuclear, hydro - chip `TURBINE`
- Solar cell - chip `DC DIRECT`

## Facts and sources
- ~4 kWh (units) per day per 1 kWp rooftop, yearly average rule of thumb: https://support.housing.com/support/solutions/articles/4000200675-how-much-electricity-can-a-rooftop-solar-pv-system-generate- (page general; widely used Indian rule of thumb)
- 1 kWh is called a "unit" in India: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kilowatt-hour
- Coal-dominated thermal >76% of India's utility generation 2023-24 (CEA): https://www.downtoearth.org.in/amp/story/energy/indias-power-shift-enters-utilisation-phase-as-renewables-surge-coal-holds-energy-backbone
- Hydro generator can start and produce power in 5-10 minutes: https://www.army.mil/article/271087/hydropower_harnessing_the_power_of_water_for_a_sustainable_future
- Pavagada Solar Park, Tumkur district, Karnataka, 2,050 MW, was the world's largest solar plant on completion: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pavagada_Solar_Park
- India reached 50% non-fossil installed capacity in 2025, five years ahead of 2030 target: https://www.energymonitor.ai/news/india-surpasses-50-non-fossil-fuel-power-capacity-target/ and https://www.deccanherald.com/india/india-hits-50-non-fossil-power-milestone-ahead-of-2030-clean-energy-target-3629888
