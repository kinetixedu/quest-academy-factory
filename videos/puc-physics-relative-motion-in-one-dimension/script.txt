D1|0.35|Where does the rule come from? One step at a time.
D2|0.75|Step one. The position of B as seen from A is the gap between them. x B A equals x B minus x A.
D3|0.75|Step two. Divide each change by the same small time, to see how fast the gap changes.
D4|0.75|Step three. Change in x B over time is v B. Change in x A over time is v A. So v B A equals v B minus v A.
D5|0.75|Step four. Swap the watcher, and only the sign flips. v A B equals minus v B A.
