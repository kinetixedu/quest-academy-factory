D1|0.35|Now, how fast does the ball go? One step at a time.
D2|0.75|Step one. In one turn, the ball covers the whole circle. That distance is the circumference, two pi r.
D3|0.75|Step two. One turn takes one time period, T. So the speed v equals two pi r divided by T.
D4|0.75|Step three. Frequency, nu, is the number of turns in each second. So nu equals one over T.
D5|0.75|Step four. Put them together. v equals two pi r nu. Metres times turns per second gives metres per second.
