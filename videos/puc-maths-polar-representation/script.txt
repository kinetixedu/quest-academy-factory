B1|0.4|Recall episode forty two. z equals a plus i b has a modulus r, how far, and an argument theta, which way.
B2|0.75|Step one. Drop a line from P down to the real axis. You get a right angled triangle, with long side r.
B3|0.75|Step two. The base is a. Cos theta is base over long side, so a equals r cos theta.
B4|0.75|Step three. The height is b. Sin theta is height over long side, so b equals r sin theta.
B5|0.75|Step four. Put both back into a plus i b. z equals r cos theta, plus i r sin theta.
B6|0.65|Take r out. z equals r, times cos theta plus i sin theta. That is the polar form.
